Built from real construction work.

Alert Tradie Pro is a practical operating platform for builders and trade teams. It was shaped around the day-to-day needs of a working construction business—not a generic software template.

01

Who we are

Alert Tradie Pro is the software platform. Alert Construction is the Melbourne construction company behind it. Together, the aim is simple: give construction teams a clearer way to run work from the first estimate through to the final handover.

02

What we do

We bring estimating, company pricing, quote review, project records, site tasks, finance, documents and reporting into one connected workspace. Owners keep the full business view, while each team member sees only what is relevant to their responsibility.

03

Why trust us

The platform is designed around real construction workflows, clear records and controlled access. Company rates stay consistent, saved estimates keep the prices used at the time, and role permissions protect sensitive project and financial information.
