Who we are

Construction experience behind every tool.

Alert Construction is a licensed unlimited builder based in Melbourne, delivering new homes, extensions and renovations from the first sketch to final handover. With engineering and architectural support working alongside construction, planning, compliance and delivery stay connected.

Alert Tradie Pro was created from the work behind those projects: setting company rates, preparing estimates, reviewing trade quotes, coordinating site teams and keeping reliable records. It brings that work into one practical platform, giving owners a clear business view while each team member sees only the tools needed for their role.
