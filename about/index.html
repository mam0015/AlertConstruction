01Who We Are
Construction first. Technology with a purpose.

Built by people who understand the job beyond the screen.

Alert Construction is a Melbourne building company working across new homes, extensions and renovations. Our projects bring builders, engineers, estimators, trades, site teams and clients into the same delivery process.

Alert Tradie Pro grew from that work. It was created to solve the practical problems that appear every day: inconsistent rates, slow estimating, scattered documents, missed follow-ups, unclear responsibilities and project information that is difficult to find when it matters.

The platform is not designed as another generic office system. It follows the way construction work actually moves—from the first plan and estimate, through site delivery, to invoicing, reporting and handover.
