Who We Are

We are Alert Construction, Alert Engineers, and Alert Tradie Pro, working together to provide complete construction, engineering, and customer project support.

Alert Construction

Manages residential construction, renovations, extensions, inspections, and on-site building work.

Alert Engineers

Provides engineering advice, assessments, inspections, designs, and technical support.

Alert Tradie Pro

Our online customer request and project-tracking portal. Customers can submit service requests, upload documents and photos, view project updates, follow the progress of their renovation or construction work, see completed stages, review schedules, receive important notices, and communicate with the project team.

Together, we combine construction experience, engineering expertise, and digital project tracking to give customers a clearer, more organised, and more transparent building experience.
