WHAT V47 CHANGES
----------------
Operation Hub navigation
  - Removes Projects, Estimates, Invoices and Site Diary from the Operation
    Hub side menu.
  - Keeps Overview, Team Management, Project Photo Timeline, Team & Roles,
    Activity History and Team Messages unchanged.
  - Keeps Financial Data as its own approved commercial section.
  - Packages Quotes, Schedule, Checklists, Documents and Reports into one clean
    Work Management group.

Quotes
  - Opens a dedicated customer quote archive, not Quote Analysis.
  - Creates folders with a freely typed customer name and work type.
  - Uploads, opens and deletes quote files inside each customer folder.
  - Stores files privately and keeps them available after changing devices.

Schedule
  - Adds an Apple Calendar-style month view.
  - Selects a date and creates a New Event with title, time, project and notes.
  - Owner/Admin/Manager can assign events to team members.
  - Other active roles can create and manage their own events.

Checklists
  - Provides a clean launch page for Site Supervisor Checklist and Permit
    Checklist without mixing the two tools.

Documents
  - Creates, renames and deletes custom folders.
  - Uploads photos, PDFs, reports, drawings, spreadsheets and other files.
  - Uses a private 25 MB-per-file storage boundary.

Reports
  - Displays the existing Team Management daily reports.
  - Shows completed work, unfinished work, problems or delays and notes for
    tomorrow.
  - Avoids creating a second, conflicting report database.

Live Project
  - Loads the active project's Cover, Featured or After photo when available.
  - Uses the verified local completed-home image only as a reliable fallback.


ROLE ACCESS
-----------
Owner / Admin / Manager
  Quote and document folders/files, all schedule events and all daily reports.

Estimator
  Quote and document folders/files, personal/assigned schedule and own reports.

Site Supervisor / Worker
  Schedule, Checklists and their authorised daily reports.
  No Quote Archive or general Documents access.

These limits are enforced by the V47 Supabase functions, Row-Level Security
boundary and private Storage policies, not only by hidden buttons.
